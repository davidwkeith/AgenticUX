# Memes (sidecar)

Pop culture shorthand for each principle in [`principles.md`](principles.md), for talks, slides, and social posts. A reference is a pitch, not a rule. The principles are the spec.

Most of these images and clips are copyrighted. Quote briefly, link to the source, and make original art where you can.
